Joseph had a question, so he read God’s word.
There he learned that he could always ask the Lord.
Joseph needed wisdom, so he knelt to pray,
Trusting God would answer him and show the way. ###
In a light above him, brighter than the sun,
Joseph saw the Father and His holy Son.
When I’m needing wisdom, I can kneel and pray.
God, who answered Joseph, answers me today. ###
I may not see a vision, but I can know and feel
That God our Heav’nly Father and His Son are real.
They always love and hear me; I know this is true.
Joseph prayed in faith, and I can too.
Yes, Joseph prayed in faith, and I can too. ###
